# Testing Skill Again in Shop

For people and agents who test the mod in the game. Check `logs\rqmod.log` for
`Loading skill-again-in-shop` first.

## What it adds

No buttons and no `rq.agent.control` ids. Do not invent controls for it.

## How to check it

1. Clear a dungeon, go back to the hub and open Frostbuy.
2. **Skill Again** is on the Special shelf, even if you already own a spent one.
3. On the first restock of a visit without one, the log says `stocked Skill Again (ii=N)`.

Buying it spends gold in the save: work on a copy.
